Ship breaking changes without breaking customers.

Parcel, a shipping API, is moving from version 2026-03-01 to 2026-09-01. Follow one customer who wrote code against the old version, first without Mender, then with a Mender adapter.

Everything runs in your browser. Parcel and its customers are made up for this demo.

    Mender writes an adapter
    
        
    From Parcel's two API specs, Mender's AI writes a small adapter: rename fields on the way in, translate answers on the way out. The AI writes it once. On live requests it's plain code, so every answer is fast and predictable.

    Replay proves it before any customer sees it
    
        
    Mender replays 200 calls recorded last week from customers still on 2026-03-01. Each call runs twice: against the old version, to learn which fields change on every run, and through the adapter into 2026-09-01. Everything else has to match what the customer originally got.
