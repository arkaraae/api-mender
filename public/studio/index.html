Make your breaking change safe to ship.

Point Mender at the old and new version of your API. It calls both, finds what changed, writes the adapter that keeps customers on the old version working, proves it, and gives you the rules to deploy.

It opens connected to the Parcel sandbox, a small test API. Swap in your own URLs to try it on your API, or paste records by hand. Your data only goes to the URLs you enter, and to Claude if you use "Write with AI".

  1. 1

    Call your API, old and new

    Mender requests the same records from both versions. Use {path} in each URL where the record path goes, and add any headers your API needs, such as a version header or a test key.

    Mender writes the adapter

    The adapter is a short list of rules: what was renamed, rescaled or reformatted, and what can't be saved. Mender runs the rules forward on requests from old customers and backward on the answers they get.

    How the rules are found. "Find rules from examples" matches fields between your two records by value: same value under a new name, the same number times 10, 100 or 1000, the same text in different case, the same moment in a different time format, and renamed values. It runs in this page with no AI. "Write with AI" asks Claude, on your own Claude account, and appears only where that's available.
